Orion Weller

I am a researcher at Google DeepMind working on Gemini, where I’ve contributed to the post-training of the Gemini 3.5 to Gemini 4 series of models (and beyond). My current research focuses on long-context capabilities, model behavior and collaboration, and multi-agent coordination.

Previously, I completed my PhD at the Center for Language and Speech Processing at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Benjamin Van Durme and Dawn Lawrie and graciously supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. My PhD research centered on the intersection of Information Retrieval (IR) and Large Language Models (LLMs), specifically exploring instruction-based and reasoning-aware retrieval (Promptriever, Rank1, LIMIT), encoder model pre-training (ModernBERT, mmBERT), and model evaluation (FollowIR, Dated Data).

I was fortunate to intern with great mentors at Meta FAIR, Google DeepMind, AI2, Samaya, and Apple. Earlier, I received my B.S. in CS and statistics from BYU, advised by Kevin Seppi and Quinn Snell.
